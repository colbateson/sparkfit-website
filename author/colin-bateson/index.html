Colin Bateson

Colin builds SparkFit, an independent UK fitness app available since March 2026, and is the named editor responsible for the articles published on this site.

His work focuses on turning broad fitness and nutrition guidance into short workouts, practical meal-planning steps and routines that fit around work and family life. Sources are linked in each article so readers can inspect the evidence directly.

Some drafts use AI assistance. Colin is responsible for the final editorial decision, corrections and keeping product claims consistent with the app. Read the editorial policy for the full process.
