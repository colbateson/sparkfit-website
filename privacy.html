Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 7, 2026

1. Data Collection & Usage

To provide the SparkFit fitness experience, we collect and process certain information depending on how you use the app:

  • Account Information: We may collect your email address and an associated account identifier to create and manage your SparkFit account and allow you to access the app across supported devices.
  • Fitness Data: SparkFit processes information such as workout logs, awards earned, meal plans, weight history, fitness preferences and other information you provide in the app in order to provide progress tracking, workout recommendations and personalised coaching.
  • Marketing Communications: If you explicitly opt in to receive marketing communications, we may use your email address to send you fitness tips, product updates and promotional offers. You can withdraw your consent at any time using the "Unsubscribe" link included in our marketing emails.

2. Cloud Syncing & Backups

SparkFit uses your device and supported cloud services to help store and synchronise your fitness information:

  • iOS Users: Fitness data and app settings may be backed up and synchronised using Apple iCloud, where this functionality is enabled and available.
  • Android Users: Fitness data and app settings may be backed up and synchronised using Google Drive, where this functionality is enabled and available.
  • Local & Cloud Data: SparkFit does not use its own servers as the primary storage location for your workout and fitness history. Where cloud backup or synchronisation is used, this information is stored within the relevant Apple or Google cloud service and is also subject to that provider's privacy, security and retention policies.

3. AI Processing

SparkFit may use artificial intelligence services to provide features such as personalised workouts, fitness guidance and AI Coach responses. Information necessary to generate a response may be securely transmitted for processing. This may include your query and relevant fitness information where required to provide a personalised response.

We do not sell your AI Coach conversations or fitness information to third-party advertisers. Information sent for AI processing is limited to what is reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

SparkFit may use trusted third-party service providers to operate, maintain and improve the app. These may include services for app distribution, authentication, subscriptions, analytics, crash reporting, cloud storage and artificial intelligence processing.

These providers may process limited device, account, diagnostic or usage information where necessary to provide their services. Any third-party processing is subject to the applicable provider's privacy policy and data protection obligations.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

SparkFit retains personal data only for as long as it is necessary to provide the app and its services, fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

  • Account Information: Your SparkFit account information, including your email address and account identifier, is retained for as long as your SparkFit account remains active. If you request deletion of your account, we will delete or anonymise the personal data associated with your SparkFit account within 30 days of receiving and processing the deletion request, unless we are legally required to retain specific information for longer.
  • Fitness Data Stored on Your Device: Fitness data stored locally on your device is retained until you delete the relevant data, delete your SparkFit account where applicable, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app may permanently remove locally stored information that has not been backed up.
  • Cloud Backup Data: Fitness information backed up to your personal Apple iCloud or Google Drive account is retained according to your cloud account settings and the retention policies of Apple or Google. You can manage or delete these backup files through the relevant cloud service. SparkFit does not control how long Apple or Google retain information within your personal cloud account after you request its deletion from those services.
  • Marketing Information: If you have opted in to marketing communications, your email address and marketing preferences are retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion. After you unsubscribe, we may retain a minimal suppression record where necessary to ensure that we honour your request and do not send further marketing communications.
  • Account Deletion: You may request deletion of your SparkFit account and the personal data associated with it at any time using the account deletion option within the app, through the account deletion method provided on our website, or by contacting us at hello@spark-fit.com. Once an account deletion request has been completed, the associated personal data controlled by SparkFit will be permanently deleted or anonymised, subject only to information we are required to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting or regulatory purposes.
  • Legally Required Retention: In limited circumstances, we may retain certain information for longer where required by applicable law, regulation, tax or accounting requirements, or where reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. When information is retained for one of these purposes, it will not be used for unrelated purposes.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. Where information is transmitted between SparkFit and external services, we use secure encrypted connections where supported.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information and the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You can exercise applicable rights through the controls provided within SparkFit or by contacting us at hello@spark-fit.com.

8. Children's Privacy

SparkFit is not intended for children under the minimum age required to consent to the processing of personal data in their country without parental or guardian authorisation. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental or guardian consent is legally required.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to SparkFit, our data practices, legal requirements or third-party services. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
