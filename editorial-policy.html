Editorial policy

How SparkFit articles are made

Who is responsible?

Colin Bateson is the named editor responsible for publication decisions and corrections on SparkFit articles.

Does SparkFit use AI?

Yes. SparkFit may use AI to help draft or organise an article. AI assistance is not treated as a source and does not remove human editorial responsibility.

How are claims checked?

Articles link to the studies and public sources used for important health, nutrition and training claims. Product features and prices are checked against SparkFit’s current app and store listings. Medical diagnosis and individual treatment advice are outside the scope of the blog.

How can I request a correction?

Email hello@spark-fit.com with the page address and the specific statement that needs review. Substantive corrections should be reflected in the article’s modified date.
