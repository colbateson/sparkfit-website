Activate your SparkFit Premium access

Your employer has invited you to use SparkFit Premium. Continue in the SparkFit app to sign in or verify your account and activate access.

BEFORE YOU OPEN THE APP

Using a work email on a work device?

Forward the original invitation email to your personal inbox, then open it on the phone where you use SparkFit. Keep the secure link intact and only send it to yourself.

  1. Open the invitation on your personal phone.
  2. Install SparkFit if needed, then open the invitation in the app.
  3. Sign in to the SparkFit account you want to receive access. It can use a different email from the work address invited.
  4. Confirm that account and tap “Activate Premium access”.
Your employer sees the invited work email and whether access is active. Your personal SparkFit email and activity are not shown to them. They cannot see your workouts, nutrition, conversations, measurements or personal progress.
