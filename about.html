About SparkFit

Short workouts for people with full lives.

SparkFit is an independent UK fitness app built by Colin and published by MYPERSONALTRAINER Limited. It has been available since March 2026.

We built it because a useful fitness plan should work around real days: work, family, travel, a crowded gym and the weeks when motivation is low. SparkFit turns that into a clear 15-minute home or gym workout, practical meal guidance and coach-style support when you need it.

We keep the product straightforward: tell SparkFit your goal, time and available equipment, then follow the plan. No hour-long sessions and no complicated programme to decode.
