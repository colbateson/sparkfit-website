SparkFit Business

Business privacy information

Effective 27 November 2026. This notice supplements the SparkFit Privacy Policy for employer pilots, subscriptions and employee access.

Information we use

For an organisation, we process the organisation name and website, billing contact name and email, seat quantity, subscription and invoice status, portal administration, and support correspondence. For an employee invitation, the employer supplies a work or contact email address so we can deliver the invitation and report its delivery and activation status. The employee signs in to a separately verified SparkFit account of their choice; its email may be different from the invited address. The employee may forward the invitation to their own personal inbox to open it on their phone. We link access to the verified SparkFit account only after the employee confirms activation in the app. We do not disclose the employee account’s email to the employer.

Who can see what

Employers can see invitation and activation status and the seat counts needed to administer access. They cannot see employee workouts, nutrition, conversations, measurements, weight or personal progress. Employee activity remains private. We do not use employer-funded access to merge or replace an employee’s personal App Store or Google Play purchase.

Service providers

Cloudflare hosts and secures the business portal and its operational database. Stripe Managed Payments processes eligible payments as merchant of record and may process billing contact, organisation, address and transaction information to collect payments, calculate and remit applicable taxes, handle refunds, disputes and transaction support. Resend delivers transactional email and reports delivery events. RevenueCat processes the app account identifier and Business entitlement state to activate or remove access. These providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy commitments.

Retention

  • Rejected or abandoned pilot applications: 12 months after decision or last activity.
  • Approved pilot applications: pilot term plus 24 months.
  • Expired or revoked invitation tokens: token hashes are removed; status metadata is retained for up to 90 days.
  • Ended employee memberships: 24 months after access ends.
  • Employer administrator access: disabled when access ends; related records retained up to 90 days unless needed for a dispute.
  • Security and administrator audit logs: 24 months.
  • Email delivery metadata: 12 months.
  • Employer billing and completed billing event records: up to 8 years, subject to legal obligations and legal holds.
  • Operational backups: up to 35 days.

We delete or anonymise information when its retention period ends unless a legal obligation, dispute or security need requires longer retention. Financial records may need to be kept for a period set by applicable accounting and tax rules.

Contact

For a privacy request about employer-funded access, email partnerships@spark-fit.com. You can also read the full SparkFit Privacy Policy.
