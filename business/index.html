UK context

Built around workplace wellbeing, without unrealistic ROI claims.

SparkFit does not promise to reduce absence, transform productivity or guarantee health outcomes. These UK sources explain the broader context for accessible workplace wellbeing support.

148.8m

working days lost

The ONS estimated 148.8 million UK working days were lost to sickness or injury in 2025, equivalent to 4.4 days per worker. The ONS labels these statistics “in development” and advises caution.

Source: Office for National Statistics, 2026 ↗
150

minutes per week

UK Chief Medical Officers recommend at least 150 minutes of moderate activity a week, while stressing that any activity is better than none and every minute counts.

Source: UK Chief Medical Officers, GOV.UK ↗
2

strength days a week

The same UK guidance recommends muscle-strengthening activity on at least two days a week. SparkFit's short sessions can help employees find practical opportunities to move.

Source: UK Chief Medical Officers' guidelines ↗
WORKPLACE

support should be inclusive

NICE recommends organisation-wide programmes that maximise opportunities for employees to participate and are developed in consultation with staff.

Source: NICE workplace guidance ↗

Questions, answered

What employers usually want to know.

Need a different detail? Speak directly with SparkFit Partnerships.

partnerships@spark-fit.com
What happens after the free trial?+

There's no automatic charge or commitment. If SparkFit is a good fit for your team, choose the access period and number of employee codes you would like to order.

Can we purchase SparkFit for more than 20 employees?+

Yes. Published pricing covers orders from 20 to 500 codes. Use the calculator for an immediate guide price. Contact SparkFit Partnerships for 501 codes or more.

How much does employee access cost?+

Employer pricing depends on the access period and number of codes. Choose 3, 6, 9 or 12 months, or lifetime access. Published volume discounts apply from 20 to 500 codes, and the calculator shows the guide total.

What happens when fixed-term access ends?+

There is no automatic renewal. Your organisation can top up access with another fixed term if you want employees to continue. Lifetime codes are a one-off purchase and do not need a top-up.

Is SparkFit already available to download?+

Yes. Employers can verify the product on the App Store and Google Play. These download links support product verification; partnership enquiries should still come through this page.

What reporting does an employer receive?+

Your organisation can receive activation reporting showing who has activated an account. It does not include individual app usage, workouts, nutrition information, conversations, measurements or progress.

How much work is required from our team?+

The model is designed to be light to manage. Your organisation agrees the access, shares the activation portal and instructions, and can review activation status. Employees use SparkFit independently.

Who can apply?+

UK organisations can enquire about a trial, annual employee wellbeing access, or a distribution/community partnership. Availability is discussed directly and an enquiry is not a guarantee of acceptance.
