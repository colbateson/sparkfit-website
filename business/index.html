How it works

From your first click to your team using SparkFit.

Follow the full journey through the business website, secure checkout, both business portals, the employee invitation and the SparkFit app.

  1. 01

    Business website

    Choose how many people to support

    Set your team size in the pricing calculator. Your annual volume price updates immediately, with applicable tax shown at checkout.

  2. 02

    Stripe Checkout

    Pay securely for the annual licences

    Review the organisation, seat count and recurring annual price. Stripe calculates applicable tax from the billing details and displays the final amount before payment.

  3. 03

    SparkFit admin · internal

    Your organisation is visible in the admin dashboard

    The SparkFit team can review organisation details, billing state, renewal timing, seat usage and provisioning issues. This private dashboard is for SparkFit staff only.

  4. 04

    Employer portal

    Sign in and see your subscription at a glance

    Your billing contact signs in with a one-time secure link. The portal shows total, active, pending and available seats, plus subscription dates and billing access.

  5. 05

    Invite · manage · reclaim

    Invite people and free seats when they leave

    Send individual or bulk invitations, check delivery and resend or correct an address. Remove employer-funded access when someone leaves; their seat becomes available again.

  6. 06

    Employee invitation

    Employees accept on their own SparkFit account

    The employee opens the secure invitation, signs in or verifies their email, then accepts access in the app. If their SparkFit account uses another email, they sign in to that account first.

    ✓The employer sees activation status only, never workouts, nutrition, messages or personal progress.
  7. 07

    SparkFit app

    Premium access opens in the employee’s app

    After activation, the Business entitlement unlocks SparkFit Premium in the employee’s own account. Their personal purchases and wellbeing data remain private if their employer access later ends.

Questions, answered

What employers usually want to know.

Need a different detail? Speak directly with SparkFit Partnerships.

partnerships@spark-fit.com
How does the annual employer subscription work?+

Choose your employee licence count on this page, then complete the annual subscription through secure Stripe Checkout. Checkout displays any applicable tax and the final amount before payment.

How are volume discounts calculated?+

The existing whole-order bands apply 20% from 20 licences, 25% from 50, 30% from 75, 35% from 100, 40% from 250 and 50% from 500. Reaching a threshold changes the price of every licence in the order.

Why is employer pricing lower than direct app purchasing?+

Employer pricing applies the published volume discount to the £20.83 annual business base price. The comparison with individual £24.99 App Store or Google Play pricing is indicative; employer Checkout adds any applicable tax to the employer price.

How is tax handled for employer orders?+

Website prices are before applicable tax. Stripe Managed Payments calculates tax based on the transaction and customer location, and shows the final amount in Checkout before you pay. The £24.99 consumer reference price is the annual App Store or Google Play price including VAT where applicable.

Do employees need to pay?+

No. The organisation funds the selected annual licences. Employees use the employer-funded activation route rather than purchasing Premium individually.

How do employees activate their licences?+

Invite employees from the employer portal. Each employee opens their secure invitation, signs in or verifies their email, and accepts access in SparkFit. They keep using their own account.

Can licences be reassigned?+

Invite and remove employees from your employer portal. An active employee or pending invitation reserves one seat. Removed employees lose employer-funded Business access; their personal account and purchases remain separate.

How do we add or remove seats?+

Seat increases take effect after Stripe confirms any prorated payment. Reductions take effect at renewal and cannot fall below the active employees plus pending invitations. Remove people or cancel pending invitations before requesting a lower seat count.

What happens when an employee leaves?+

Remove the employee from your employer portal. This ends only employer-funded Business access; the employee keeps their personal SparkFit account and personal purchases. You can then reuse the seat.

What happens if the employer cancels?+

You can turn off renewal in your employer billing portal before the renewal date. Access continues until the end of the paid term. Employer-funded access then ends; personal accounts and purchases are unaffected. Read the Business subscription terms.

What if an employee already pays for Premium?+

Employer-funded access is separate from a personal App Store or Google Play subscription. The employee should contact SparkFit before activation so overlapping paid access can be reviewed. Do not assume an app-store subscription is cancelled automatically.

How do we request more than 500 seats?+

Use the Contact Partnerships link or enquiry form for tailored pricing. Online orders are limited to 500 seats.

How does the 30-day free pilot work?+

Eligible UK organisations can request full SparkFit Premium access for up to 20 employees for 30 days. No payment details are required, there is no obligation to continue and the pilot does not convert automatically to a paid subscription.

Is SparkFit already available to download?+

Yes. Employers can verify the product on the App Store and Google Play. These download links support product verification; partnership enquiries should still come through this page.

What reporting does an employer receive?+

Your organisation can receive activation reporting showing who has activated an account. It does not include individual app usage, workouts, nutrition information, conversations, measurements or progress.
