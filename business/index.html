Questions, answered

What employers usually want to know.

Need a different detail? Speak directly with SparkFit Partnerships.

partnerships@spark-fit.com
How does the annual employer subscription work?+

Your organisation chooses the number of employee licences and receives a volume discount. Each licence funds one active employee's SparkFit Premium access for a year. The current website sends a priced enquiry for confirmation and does not take payment.

How are volume discounts calculated?+

The existing whole-order bands apply 20% from 20 licences, 25% from 50, 30% from 75, 35% from 100, 40% from 250 and 50% from 500. Reaching a threshold changes the price of every licence in the order.

Why is employer pricing lower than direct app purchasing?+

Employer pricing applies the published volume discount to the annual consumer reference price excluding VAT. The calculator compares like-for-like totals so the displayed saving reflects the volume price rather than a VAT mismatch.

Why does the consumer reference price include VAT?+

The public £24.99 annual App Store and Google Play price is a consumer price and includes VAT. Employer discounts are calculated from its £20.83 VAT-exclusive equivalent.

What does the VAT toggle change?+

It changes every displayed estimate, including package prices, direct comparisons, annual totals, monthly equivalents, per-employee costs and savings. It uses an illustrative 20% UK VAT rate. Actual tax will be determined by the future checkout and server-side tax system.

Do employees need to pay?+

No. The organisation funds the selected annual licences. Employees use the employer-funded activation route rather than purchasing Premium individually.

How do employees activate their licences?+

Your organisation shares the activation instructions supplied by SparkFit Partnerships. Employees activate their own SparkFit accounts and use the app independently.

Can licences be reassigned?+

Licence reassignment and a self-service employer dashboard are planned. During the current rollout, discuss changes with SparkFit Partnerships so the available process can be confirmed.

How do we add or remove seats?+

Automated subscription quantity changes and proration are planned for the Stripe Billing integration. They are not available through this website yet. Contact SparkFit Partnerships to discuss a change.

What happens when an employee leaves?+

The employee retains their personal SparkFit account and data. The process for removing employer-funded Premium access and reassigning the licence must currently be coordinated with SparkFit Partnerships.

What happens if the employer cancels?+

Cancellation terms will be confirmed in the employer agreement. Automatic annual renewal is planned but is not currently active on this website. Employees keep their personal accounts, but employer-funded Premium access would end according to the agreed subscription terms.

What if an employee already pays for Premium?+

Employer-funded access is separate from a personal App Store or Google Play subscription. The employee should contact SparkFit before activation so overlapping paid access can be reviewed. Do not assume an app-store subscription is cancelled automatically.

How do we request more than 1,000 seats?+

Use the Contact Partnerships link or the enquiry form for tailored enterprise pricing. The calculator is intentionally limited to 1,000 seats.

How does the 30-day free pilot work?+

Eligible UK organisations can request full SparkFit Premium access for up to 20 employees for 30 days. No payment details are required, there is no obligation to continue and the pilot does not convert automatically to a paid subscription.

Is SparkFit already available to download?+

Yes. Employers can verify the product on the App Store and Google Play. These download links support product verification; partnership enquiries should still come through this page.

What reporting does an employer receive?+

Your organisation can receive activation reporting showing who has activated an account. It does not include individual app usage, workouts, nutrition information, conversations, measurements or progress.
