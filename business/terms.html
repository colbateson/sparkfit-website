SparkFit Business

Employer subscription terms

Effective 27 November 2026. These terms apply to annual employer-funded SparkFit Premium access purchased through SparkFit Business. They do not replace rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Subscription and price

The minimum online order is 20 seats and the maximum is 500. Larger orders require a written quote from SparkFit Partnerships. Each seat provides one employee with employer-funded Premium access for a 12-month term. The agreed base price is £20.83 per seat per year before the published volume discount and applicable tax.

Stripe Managed Payments processes eligible transactions as merchant of record and calculates, collects and remits applicable transaction taxes. Prices shown on the SparkFit website are before applicable tax. Checkout shows the tax and final amount due before payment.

Renewal and cancellation

The subscription begins after the first successful payment and renews automatically for a further 12 months unless the organisation cancels before renewal. SparkFit will send renewal reminders around 30 and 7 days before renewal and notify the billing contact of price changes at least 30 days in advance. An organisation can switch off renewal in the employer billing portal at any time before the renewal charge. Access continues until the end of the paid term; cancellation does not automatically create a refund.

Payment failure

If a renewal payment fails, Stripe may retry it for up to 14 days. Employer-funded access remains available during the initial 14-day grace period. If the invoice remains unpaid, access is suspended after that grace period. If it remains unpaid for 30 days, the subscription may be ended and employer-funded access removed. Paying the outstanding invoice restores eligible access. Personal App Store or Google Play purchases are separate and are not changed.

Refunds

Free pilots are not refundable. Paid annual subscriptions are normally non-refundable after employee access begins. A full refund is available for a duplicate charge, a charge caused by a SparkFit billing error, or a charge taken after a correctly recorded cancellation. Within 14 days of the organisation’s first purchase, a full cancellation and refund may be requested if no employee invitation has been activated. Any exceptional partial refund after activation requires manual review. Refunds are made to the original payment method, subject to applicable law. Mid-term seat removals do not produce a cash refund.

Seat changes and employee access

Seat increases take effect after Stripe confirms any required prorated payment. Seat reductions take effect at the next annual renewal and do not create a mid-term refund or credit. The renewal quantity cannot be lower than the number of active employees plus pending invitations; remove employees or cancel pending invitations first. Employers may remove an employee and reuse that seat during the paid term. Removal ends only employer-funded Business access; it does not remove the employee’s personal account or personal purchase.

Privacy boundary

The employer portal reports invitations and access activation status only. Employers cannot view employee workouts, nutrition, conversations, measurements, weight or personal progress. See the Business privacy information and SparkFit Privacy Policy.

For billing or account help, contact partnerships@spark-fit.com.
