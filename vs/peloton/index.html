SparkFit vs the Peloton App

Peloton is built around a large class library and instructors. SparkFit is built around one 15-minute workout, a food plan and the next useful step.

What mattersSparkFitPeloton App
Workout styleA clear 15-minute home or one-zone gym circuit.Instructor-led strength, cardio, running, cycling, yoga, Pilates and meditation classes.
Choosing todayPick home or gym, then follow the plan.Browse classes, programmes, instructors and durations.
NutritionMacros, personalised meal plans, recipes and shopping lists.The public app listing focuses on workouts and tracking, not meal planning.
Coaching24/7 AI coach using your plan and targets.Instructor motivation, programmes, challenges and live/on-demand classes.
Best fitYou want fewer decisions and food support in the same app.You enjoy choosing from a deep class library and following instructors.

Choose SparkFit if a smaller, clearer plan beats a bigger library.
