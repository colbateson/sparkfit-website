SparkFit vs Sweat

Both combine home or gym workouts with food support. The useful difference is how much choice, time and guidance you want.

What mattersSparkFitSweat
WorkoutsFocused 15-minute home workouts and practical one-zone gym circuits.A broad library of programmes across strength, HIIT, yoga, Pilates, recovery and more.
FoodPersonalised macros, family-friendly meal plans, recipes and a shopping list.More than 200 meals plus nutrition and wellbeing education.
CoachingA 24/7 AI coach that answers using your plan, equipment and targets.Programmes and cues from named trainers, plus community support.
Pricing shape24-hour no-card trial; monthly, annual and £59.99 lifetime options.Monthly or yearly subscription after an eligible free trial.
Best fitYou want one short workout and one clear food plan without browsing a large library.You want lots of programmes, named trainers and a large women-focused community.
